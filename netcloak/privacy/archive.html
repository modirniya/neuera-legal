Version 2025-11-12

Current Version
November 12, 2025
Changes in this version:

Major Service Update - Updated privacy policy to reflect the new Free Tier model with data quota tracking and enhanced transparency features.

Key Changes:
  • Added device permissions section (Internet, VPN service, Foreground service, Notifications, Advertising ID)
  • Updated data collection with client-side monitoring (every 10 seconds) and server-side verification (every 30 seconds)
  • Added DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) via Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 for secure server discovery
  • Revised session model to 24-hour sessions with 1.05GB data quota
  • Added data extension feature (500MB per ad viewed)
  • Implemented grace period (5 minutes after data limit exceeded)
  • Added proactive warning system (15% Low, 5% Critical alerts)
  • Enhanced privacy commitments with data quota transparency

Effective Date: November 12, 2025

Archived

Changes in this version:

Initial Privacy Policy Release - This is the first version of our privacy policy, establishing our core privacy commitments and practices.

Key Features:

Future Versions

Future updates to our privacy policy will be listed here. We are committed to transparency and will document all changes with clear explanations of what has been modified and why.

Our Update Process:
  1. Review: We regularly review our privacy practices and legal requirements
  2. Update: When changes are needed, we update the policy with a new version
  3. Notification: Users are notified through in-app notifications

  5. Acceptance: Users must accept updated policies before continuing to use the service
