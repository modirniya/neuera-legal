About Kalum

App Overview

Kalum is a prepaid Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) calling app that enables you to make outbound voice calls to telephone numbers in over 216 countries. Perfect for staying connected with family and friends abroad without expensive international calling plans.

Core Features

  • Outbound voice calls to 216+ countries
  • Prepaid credit system (no subscriptions)
  • Transparent per-minute rates shown before calling
  • Call history and transaction records
  • Manually added contacts for quick dialing
  • Credits never expire

Pricing

  • Starter: $2.00
  • Standard: $10.00
  • Premium: $25.00

Rates vary by destination. All call attempts (including unanswered) are billed per minute, rounded up.

Platform

  • iOS and Android
  • Phone number verification (Firebase)
  • Secure Stripe payments — Apple Pay & Google Pay supported
  • Twilio-powered calling

Important Notice

Kalum cannot be used for emergency calls (911, 112, 999, etc.). Always maintain access to a traditional phone service for emergencies.
