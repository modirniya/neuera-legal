About Kalum

App Overview

Kalum is a prepaid Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) calling app that enables you to make outbound voice calls to telephone numbers in approximately 200 countries. Perfect for staying connected with family and friends abroad without expensive international calling plans.

Core Features

  • Outbound voice calls to ~200 countries
  • Prepaid credit system (no subscriptions)
  • Transparent per-minute rates shown before calling
  • Call history and transaction records
  • Saved contacts (manual entry or opt-in import)
  • Credits never expire

Pricing (iOS)

  • Quick Call: $2.99 → 209 credits
  • Casual Caller: $12.99 → 909 credits
  • Frequent Calls: $29.99 → 2,099 credits

Android and web prices and credit amounts may differ slightly because Stripe's processing model is different from Apple's. The amount shown in-app at the moment of purchase is authoritative. Rates vary by destination. All call attempts (including unanswered) are billed per minute, rounded up.

Platform

  • iOS and Android
  • Phone number verification (Firebase)
  • iOS in-app purchases via Apple App Store
  • Android & web payments via Stripe (cards, Google Pay)
  • Twilio-powered calling

Important Notice

Kalum cannot be used for emergency calls (911, 112, 999, etc.). Always maintain access to a traditional phone service for emergencies.

Our Commitment
