4. The Service

Kalum is a prepaid Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) calling service that enables you to place outbound voice calls to telephone numbers worldwide.

4.1 What Kalum Provides

  • Outbound voice calls to approximately 200 destination countries routed via the public switched telephone network through our partner Twilio. The current list of supported destinations and per-minute rates is shown in the app.
  • Per-minute prepaid calling with no subscriptions and no contracts.
  • Per-call rate display before you connect.
  • Call history and transaction history within the app.
  • Credit purchases through the App Store on iOS and through Stripe (cards and Google Pay) on Android and the web.

4.2 What Kalum Does Not Provide

5. Billing and Payment

5.1 Prepaid Credit Model

Kalum is a strict prepaid service. You must purchase credits before placing a call. Credits are added to your account immediately on successful payment.

5.2 Credit Packages

Credits are sold in three tiers. On iOS, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; on Android and the web, purchases are processed by Stripe. The credit amount you receive is shown next to each tier in the app before purchase and reflects the platform's processing share.

Android and web prices and credit amounts may differ slightly because Stripe's processing model is different from Apple's. The price and credit amount displayed in-app at the moment of purchase is authoritative. Prices are listed in U.S. dollars and may be displayed in your local currency by your platform's payment system. Currency conversion and applicable taxes are handled by Apple or Stripe as the case may be.

5.3 Call Rates

  • Per-minute rates vary by destination and are displayed in the app before you initiate a call.
  • We may revise rates at any time to reflect changes from our underlying carriers. New rates apply only to calls placed after the rate change. The rate shown in the app at the moment you start a call is the rate that will be applied to that call.

5.4 How Calls Are Billed

  • Connected calls are billed in one-minute increments, rounded up to the next whole minute. A 1-second call is billed at one minute; a 121-second call is billed at three minutes.
  • A minimum of one minute is charged for any connected call.
  • Credits are deducted after the call ends; we do not reserve credits during the call.
  • All call attempts are billed, including unanswered, busy, and failed attempts, because our underlying carrier (Twilio) charges us per attempt regardless of answer state. The amount billed for an unanswered call is the one-minute minimum at the destination's rate.
  • Calls to destinations not listed in our rate table are billed at a fallback rate of $0.50 per minute. If you encounter this rate for a destination you believe should be listed, please contact hello@neuera.app.

5.5 Minimum Balance to Place a Call

To place a call, your account balance must be at least 1.5 times the per-minute rate for that destination (i.e., enough credit for ninety seconds of conversation). This is a safety margin against fraud and against running out of credit during a billable minute.

5.6 Credits Do Not Expire

Purchased credits remain in your account indefinitely as long as your account remains active. Credits are not transferable, have no cash value, and may not be exchanged for cash except as required by applicable law or as expressly provided in Section 6.

5.7 Payment Methods

  • iOS in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple charges the payment method on file with your Apple ID. We never receive your card details.
  • Android and the web use Stripe for payment processing, including support for credit and debit cards and Google Pay where available. We never receive your full card number, expiry, or CVV; your payment information is submitted directly to Stripe.
