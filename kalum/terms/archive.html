Version 2026-05-04

Current Version

Effective Date: May 4, 2026

Status: Active

Summary: Aligned the Terms with the current product. Updated credit packages to a 3-tier structure ($2 / $10 / $25), corrected the call-billing section to reflect that all attempts are billed (including unanswered calls), corrected the minimum-balance gate to 1.5× the per-minute rate, expanded refund and consumer-protection language for EEA/UK users, added an Apple/Google platform additional-terms section, and tightened anti-fraud and prohibited-use clauses.

Key Points:
  • Credit packages updated to $2 / $10 / $25 (3-tier)
  • All call attempts are billed, including unanswered, busy, and failed
  • Minimum balance to start a call: 1.5× the per-minute rate
  • Up to 2 concurrent active calls per account
  • New-account restrictions on certain high-risk destinations (24h)
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay disclosed as payment methods
  • EEA/UK 14-day right of withdrawal acknowledged
  • Liability cap floor of USD $100
  • Apple App Store / Google Play platform-additional-terms section added
  • Coverage expanded to 216+ destination countries
