RPS Mafia

Mafia, played out loud. Five to twelve players.

A free social deduction game played by voice in the browser. Reserve a seat at a scheduled game night or start a private table with a code. Voice is relayed live and never recorded, and the state of a game exists only while it is being played. 13+.
