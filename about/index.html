About

An independent app studio.

NeuEra Apps designs, ships, and maintains a small portfolio of mobile and web apps. The studio is independently held, has no outside funding, and answers to no growth calendar.

What we do

We build apps people actually use — in privacy, gaming, productivity, and communication. Four shipped today, each maintained on its own timeline. We add new things when they’re ready, not on a schedule.

How we work

Three things shape the work, and we’d rather under-promise than wave at slogans.

Independent

No outside funding. No growth targets dictated by investors. We choose what to build and when to ship it.

Long-lived

Apps stay maintained. Updates happen because they’re needed, not because a release calendar said so.

Built carefully

We’d rather ship a small number of well-considered things than chase every category. The portfolio stays small on purpose.
