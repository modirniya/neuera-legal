Privacy Policy Version History

This page contains an archive of all versions of the Odo Privacy Policy. We maintain complete version history for transparency. The current version is always available at the main privacy policy page.

Version 2025-11-28

Current Version

Effective Date: November 28, 2025

Status: Active

Summary: Updated privacy policy reflecting app rename to "Odo" and addition of optional location snapshots feature.

Key Points:
  • App renamed from "Odometer" to "Odo"
  • Added optional location snapshots (one-time GPS capture, not tracking)
  • All data stored locally in SQLite database
  • OpenStreetMap added for map display
  • Location permission is optional - app works without it
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No ads
  • iOS and Android support (Flutter)

About Our Version History

We believe in transparency. Every time we update our privacy policy, we:

  • Create a permanent archived copy of the previous version
  • Update the effective date on the new version
  • List all versions on this page for your reference
  • Maintain this archive indefinitely

Our core principle remains unchanged: your data stays on your device and we don't collect it.
