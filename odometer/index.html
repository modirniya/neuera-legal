Odo

A simple mileage tracker. Local-first.

Odo records trips and mileage. All data stays in a local SQLite database on your device — we don’t run analytics, telemetry, or ads. GPS snapshots are optional and one-shot. Built with Flutter.

Android Free No ads
Get it on Google Play
Features

Mileage, kept simple.

  • Local-first storage

    Trips are stored in an on-device SQLite database. Nothing leaves your phone.

  • Optional GPS snapshots

    One-shot location capture, not background tracking. Location permission is optional.

  • OpenStreetMap

    Maps are rendered from OpenStreetMap data.

  • No analytics, no telemetry

    No crash reporting, no usage tracking, no third-party SDKs.

  • No ads

    Free, with no advertising. The app does its job and nothing else.

  • Built with Flutter

    Native-feeling Android app today, with shared code ready to extend to other platforms.
