    Initial Terms of Use for Salon Ledger, covering its closed pilot. The agreement is with the salon owner, who invites techs to use it. The service is provided as-is during the pilot, features may change, and there is no fee. The owner is responsible for the accuracy of tickets and totals, for how techs are paid, and for having the right to enter techs' names and work records. Owner corrections take precedence over tech edits, and every change is kept in the ticket's history.
