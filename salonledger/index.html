Salon Ledger

The service-ticket pad, on the techs' own phones.

Service tickets for nail salons. Techs log each ticket on their own phone — services, prices and an optional tip — even when the phone is offline. The owner sees every ticket, corrects what needs correcting, closes the pay period and exports each tech's totals. No payments are processed and nothing is recorded about the salon's customers. Currently in a closed pilot.
