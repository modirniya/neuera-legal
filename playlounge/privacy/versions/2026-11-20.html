1. Introduction

Play Lounge ("Play Lounge", "we", "us", or "our") is operated by NeuEra Apps LLC ("NeuEra Apps"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our multiplayer board game platform, available as a web application at playlounge.live and as native apps for Android (live.playlounge.android) and iOS (live.playlounge.ios).

Play Lounge is a free multiplayer gaming platform. The only promotions we show are for our own other apps, served by us — there are no third-party ad networks. We are committed to protecting your privacy and have designed our service to collect virtually no personal information. You can enjoy our games completely anonymously without creating an account.

PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN: You can play Play Lounge without an account, and most people do. To run the game, and to count whether people come back, we use a few short-lived technical identifiers, described below. If you are 13 or older, you can choose to sign in with Google or Apple on our website, playlounge.live. That creates a Play Lounge account, which keeps your record (how many games you won, drew and lost in each game) and your running score with other signed-in players on every device you sign in on. The Android and iOS apps don't offer sign-in yet. Other players never see your name, email or photo: they see only a game name we make up for you, like “Brave Otter 42”. Apart from that optional account, the only personal details we ever receive are the ones you type into our support form.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our multiplayer gaming service. Here's exactly what we collect and don't collect:

What We Collect Directly:

  • Random Player ID: Our server creates one when you connect. It is not linked to who you are. It is kept for up to 24 hours so you can reconnect to a game.
  • Session Token: Lets you reconnect for up to 24 hours. On the web it is kept for the browser tab. In the Android and iOS apps it is kept on your device until it expires.
  • Room Codes: 6-character codes for private games. They expire after 5 minutes of inactivity.
  • Connection data: Your IP address and browser or app version (user agent) reach our server when you connect. We use the IP address in memory to count daily visitors and to protect the service from abuse. Our server never writes the IP address or user agent to its logs (see Section 5). We never use them to work out where you are.
  • Usage counts: The app tells our server when certain things happen, for example a game against the computer finished, an invite was shared, or the install prompt was dismissed. We store only daily totals per platform (web, Android, iOS). Nothing is stored per player.
  • Return counter (a random device identifier): To learn whether people come back to Play Lounge, the app and the website store a random identifier on your device and send it to our server when the app connects. It is a random number: it is not derived from your device, your network address, or anything about you, it is not an account, and it is never shared with anyone else. Our server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, the platform (web, Android or iOS), the first and last day it was seen, and whether it came back within 7 and 28 days. We use this solely to count how many visitors are new and how many return. The record is deleted 90 days after your last visit, and clearing the app's or site's data on your device removes the identifier (see Sections 5 and 9).
  • Support messages: If you use our support form: the name and email you enter, your message, and your browser or app version (see Section 6).

If you create an account (optional, ages 13 and up, on playlounge.live):

You sign in with Google or with Apple. The sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service of Google LLC that processes the data on our behalf.

What Firebase (Google) holds for us:

  • Email address. The one on your Google or Apple account. With Apple's “Hide My Email” it is a relay address Apple creates.
  • Name and profile photo. As your Google or Apple account provides them. Apple shares a name only if you allow it.
  • Firebase user ID. A random identifier Firebase assigns to your account.
  • Sign-in records. Your IP address and browser type (user agent) when you sign in. Firebase uses them to secure sign-in and prevent abuse.

What our own server holds:

  • your Firebase user ID;
  • which provider you used (Google or Apple);
  • when the account was created, when it was last used (updated at most once a day), and when it last signed out (so older sign-ins stop working);
  • the saved score (“series”) between you and each person you played in a private (invite) game while you were both signed in. For each pair this is only the two Firebase user IDs, each side's wins, the draws, the number of games, and when it was last updated;
  • a game name made up for you from fixed lists of words, like “Brave Otter 42”. You can ask for another one at any time, but you can't type one, so it never contains anything about you. People you play see it during a game and in their list of saved scores;
  • your record: for each game, how many games you won, lost, drew or left early against other players, and how many you won, lost or drew against the computer at each level (easy, medium, hard). Totals only: not a list of games, not whom you played or when. Games against other players are counted by our server when they end; games against the computer run in your browser, which reports each result while you are signed in. When you first sign in on a device, the record that device kept while you were signed out is added to your account once;
  • for each such addition, a one-way fingerprint of a random code the device made for it, so that it is added only once. It identifies nothing about the device;
  • after you delete your account: a one-way fingerprint of the deleted account's ID and the deletion time, kept for 30 days. It is used only so that a device still signed in can't silently re-create the account you just deleted.

Sign-in pages: the sign-in pages load from our address (playlounge.live/__/auth/…). Our server relays them to Firebase without your IP address, and without reading, storing or logging them. With Apple, Apple's reply passes through this relay; the first time, it includes the name and email you chose to share. Google and Apple still see your IP address on their own sign-in pages.

We do not store your email, name or photo on our server. The website shows your name and email to you, and only to you, on your account screen, read from Firebase in your browser. Other players see none of it. They see your made-up game name if you are signed in, and “Friend” or “Opponent” if you are not. A saved score does show two players that they have played each other before; it reveals nothing else about either of them. A game name can't be searched for, and nobody can contact you through it.

Age check before sign-in:

When you tap “Sign in”, we first ask the year you were born. Your answer stays in your browser and is never sent to us or to Google. If you are under 13, you can't create an account, and you keep playing exactly as before. Your browser then remembers, for one year, only that sign-in isn't available, not your birth year. During that time we don't offer or suggest signing in. Nothing about the game changes for players without an account.

What We DO NOT Collect:

  • Your name, email address or any personal identification, unless you choose to create an account (see above) or give them to us through the support form or by email
  • Passwords. We have none: an account uses Google or Apple sign-in, and no signup is needed to play
  • Location data of any kind, precise or approximate
  • Your device's own identifiers: IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID, or any identifier assigned by your phone or operating system (the return counter above is a random number we create, not one of these)
  • Contact lists or phone numbers
  • Photos, videos, or camera access
  • Audio recordings
  • Browsing history or activity outside Play Lounge
  • Payment information (we don't sell anything)
  • Text chat or message content (we have no text chat feature)

Browser and Device Storage:

We use no cookies. The app keeps a few small items in your browser or app storage: your session token and the game or room you are joining (to reconnect you), the code of a room you created in the last 10 minutes, whether you dismissed the install prompt, the random return-counter identifier described above, and, on the website only, a cached list of our apps for the banner and which of them you have tapped (so you are not shown it again). The session token and room codes are sent to our server so we can reconnect you, and the return-counter identifier so we can count returning visitors. The rest never leaves your device. Clearing site data, or uninstalling the app, removes it all.

With or without an account, your browser (or the app) keeps your record: how many games you won, drew and lost in each game, against people and against the computer at each level, and how many days in a row you played. It stays on your device and is sent to us only if you sign in, to add it to your account. Signing out or deleting the account doesn't remove it; clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app does.

If you sign in on our website, your browser also keeps:

  • a marker that you are signed in, so the site can reconnect your account without asking again;
  • Firebase's own sign-in data, in the browser's IndexedDB;
  • the day the “keep your score” suggestion was last shown, so it appears at most once a day.

If you answered the age check as under 13, it keeps the date until which sign-in stays unavailable (one year). Signing out or deleting the account removes the signed-in marker, the suggestion date and Firebase's sign-in data.

3. Promotions for Our Other Apps

On the website, Play Lounge shows a small banner promoting other apps we make, such as Git Game, Kalum and Odo. We build and serve it ourselves. It is not shown in the Android and iOS apps.

NO AD NETWORKS: Play Lounge contains no third-party advertising SDKs — no Google AdMob, no AdSense, no ad network of any kind. Nothing on the page is loaded from an advertising server, and no advertising identifier is read or transmitted.

How the Banner Works:

  • What it downloads: a short list of our apps (name, icon, tagline, store link).
  • What it reports: when the banner shows an app, and when someone taps it, your browser tells our server which app, the platform (web, Android browser or iPhone browser), and where it was tapped. We keep only daily totals per app, and use them to decide which of our apps to show more often. To filter out fake clicks we use a scrambled form of your IP address that changes every day, kept in memory for 30 minutes and never stored.
  • No advertising ID, no profile, no third-party pixels or cookies. We don't learn whether you install anything.

If You Tap It:

Tapping the banner opens the Google Play Store or Apple App Store listing for that app. From that point the relevant store's own privacy policy applies, exactly as it would if you had searched for the app yourself.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal information we collect for these purposes only:

  • Provide Gaming Service: Enable real-time multiplayer gameplay through WebSocket connections
  • Session Management: Maintain your game state and allow reconnection if you lose connection briefly
  • Matchmaking: Connect players for random matchmaking (anonymously, no profiles created)
  • Private Rooms: Enable private games with friends using room codes
  • Cross-Platform Play: Allow web and mobile players to play together seamlessly
  • Service Improvement: Identify and fix bugs, optimize performance
  • Security: Prevent abuse, cheating, and denial-of-service attacks
  • Counting who comes back: The return counter tells us how many visitors are new and how many return, so we know whether Play Lounge is worth people's time. It is used for nothing else.

Account data is used only to:

  • sign you in and keep you signed in on your devices;
  • keep your record, and your saved score with other signed-in players, across devices and updates, and show them to you (and only to you);
  • protect the sign-in from abuse (Firebase's use of IP address and user agent);
  • delete everything when you ask.

We never use account data for advertising, marketing emails or profiling, and we never sell it or share it for anyone else's use. We don't email you.

WE DO NOT: Track your behavior, build user profiles, or use your data for marketing purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

For kids: You can play without telling us who you are. If you're under 13, you can't make an account. Nobody you play against can see who you are or send you messages.

Play Lounge is directed in part to children. Under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) it is a “mixed audience” service: children may use it, but they are not its only or main audience. We treat every player who hasn't passed the age check as possibly under 13.

  • Playing without an account uses only the random player ID, session token, connection data and return counter described in Section 2. They serve only to run, secure and maintain the game, and to count visits and returning visitors in aggregate. COPPA calls this “support for internal operations” and allows it without parental consent. These identifiers are random. They are never linked to a name, an email or, for anyone who hasn't passed the age check, an account. They are never shown to other players and never used for advertising, profiling or contacting anyone. Our logs contain no IP address or user agent.
  • Accounts are for ages 13 and up, on our website only. Before anyone can sign in, we ask their birth year openly, with no answer preselected. Under-13s can't create an account, and their answer is never sent to us or to Google. In the game, no sign-in code from Google loads until someone on that browser has passed the age check and chosen to sign in. The account-deletion page (playlounge.live/account/delete) is the one exception: it loads the sign-in code when opened, so that an existing account can be deleted without an age check.
  • Shared devices. A browser where someone has signed in stays signed in. Anyone using it plays on that account and adds to its record and saved scores. If you share a device with a child, sign out when you're done.
  • No contact between players. There is no chat, and nothing a player types reaches another player. A signed-in player appears to others under a game name we make up from fixed word lists (like “Brave Otter 42”); nobody can type a name, so a child sees no name an adult chose. Players who aren't signed in, which includes every child under 13, appear as “Friend” or “Opponent”.
  • Support messages. If a child sends us a support message, we use the email address only to reply once, and then delete it.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete the account, its saved scores and the Firebase user, as described in Section 9, without asking for anything more.
  • Parents and guardians can ask us what we hold about their child, ask us to delete it, or ask us to stop any further use. Without an account there is nothing we can link to a particular child; a support message can be found by the email address it was sent from.
  • Our data-retention policy for children's information is the table in Section 5 (the full written policy is at Play Lounge Data Retention Policy). For each item it says what it is, why we keep it, and when it is deleted. Nothing about a child is kept indefinitely: only daily totals are kept, and they hold nothing about any individual. We also maintain a written information-security program.
  • Contact: NeuEra Apps LLC, 1968 S. Coast Hwy #2044, Laguna Beach, CA 92651, (818) 641-0005, hello@neuera.app.

Promotions & Children:

Because Play Lounge carries no third-party advertising, the usual children's-advertising concerns do not arise: no advertising identifier is read, no profile is built, and no ad network receives anything about your child. The only promotions shown are for our own apps, they are the same for every player, and they are not targeted in any way.

8. Data Security

Although we collect minimal data, we still implement strong security measures:

  • Encrypted Connections: All communication between your device and our server uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • WebSocket Security: Secure WebSocket (WSS) connections for real-time gameplay
  • Short-Lived Storage: Data expires on the schedule in Section 5, which reduces the risk of data breaches
  • Server Security: Fly.io infrastructure provides DDoS protection and secure hosting
  • Token Expiration: Session tokens automatically expire after 24 hours
  • Room Code Expiration: Temporary room codes expire after 5 minutes
  • Minimal Database: Without an account, our database holds nothing that identifies you. With one, it holds only what Section 2 lists: never an email, name, photo or password

BEST SECURITY IS NO DATA: Our security strategy is simple - we store as little as we can. Even with an account, your email, name and photo stay with Google Firebase and never reach our server.

9. Your Choices & Controls

You have several options to control your privacy:

Advertising Controls:

There is nothing to opt out of. Play Lounge uses no ad networks and reads no advertising identifier, so personalised advertising, ad tracking and advertising-ID resets simply do not apply here. The banner promoting our own apps is the same for everyone and is not based on anything about you.

Data Deletion:

  • Automatic: Data expires on the schedule in Section 5
  • Manual: Clear your browser's localStorage/sessionStorage in browser settings
  • Complete Removal: Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Return counter: Clearing site data or uninstalling the app removes the identifier, and the server record expires 90 days after your last visit; email hello@neuera.app if you want it removed sooner, and tell us the day you last played so we can find it
  • Support messages: Email hello@neuera.app to have a support message deleted sooner

Deleting your account:

You can delete your account at any time:

  • on playlounge.live: tap your account icon, then Delete account, confirm, and sign in once more with Google or Apple to prove it's you;
  • on any device, at https://playlounge.live/account/delete: sign in with the account you want to delete, confirm twice, and it's gone. No app is needed;
  • by email: write to hello@neuera.app from the address on the account, or tell us how you signed in. We may ask you to sign in once more to confirm the account is yours. We act within 30 days.

What deletion removes:

  • Our server: the account, your record, and every saved score it is part of, at once. Every browser signed in to the account is signed out. Copies in our host's disk backups disappear within 5 days.
  • Google Firebase: your Firebase user, with its email, name, photo and user ID. If Google can't be reached, our server keeps only the Firebase user ID in a deletion queue and retries for several hours. If it still fails, we complete the deletion by hand within 30 days. Google removes any remaining copies on its own schedule.
  • Not removed: the record each of your devices keeps for itself (Section 2, browser storage). It is not part of the account; clear the site's data or uninstall the app to remove it.
  • Apple: if you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke Play Lounge's access to your Apple ID, and Play Lounge then no longer appears under “Sign in with Apple” in your Apple ID settings.
  • Google: your Google account itself is untouched. Play Lounge's access ends because its Firebase user no longer exists. You can also remove it under “Third-party apps & services” in your Google Account.

Deleting an account doesn't affect anonymous play: you can keep playing without an account straight away.

Signing out keeps the account but ends the sign-in in that browser.

Cookies:

We use no cookies. The app keeps a few small items in your browser or app storage, listed under “Browser and Device Storage” in Section 2. The session token and room codes are sent to our server so we can reconnect you, and the return-counter identifier so we can count returning visitors. The rest never leaves your device. Clearing site data, or uninstalling the app, removes it all.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have legal privacy rights. Because we collect virtually no personal information, most of these rights don't apply in practice, but we respect them nonetheless:

General Rights:

  • Right to Access: Request information about what data we have (answer: the short-lived data in Section 5, and any support message you sent us)
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data (most of it expires on its own on the schedule in Section 5; you can delete an account yourself, see Section 9; we delete support messages on request)
  • Right to Portability: Download your data (not applicable - no stored personal data)
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing (you can stop using the service anytime)
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data collection (close the app or uninstall)

Regional Privacy Laws:

  • EU Residents (GDPR): Rights to access, deletion, portability, restriction, objection
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): Rights to know, delete, opt-out of sale (we don't sell data)
  • UK Residents (UK GDPR): Same rights as EU GDPR
  • Canadian Residents (PIPEDA): Rights to access and correction
  • Brazilian Residents (LGPD): Rights to access, correction, deletion

Exercising Your Rights:

To exercise any privacy rights, contact us at hello@neuera.app. We will respond within 30 days. Without an account, most requests will receive the response: "No personal data stored." If you have an account, you can delete it yourself at any time (Section 9).
